About Me

I’m switching careers and moving into web development while continuing to build on my background in networking. I work best when I can take an idea, build something real with it, and figure out how the pieces work together instead of only learning them in isolation. Right now, I’m developing a D&D virtual tabletop extension while also building the campaign and systems that support it. Working on the project has pushed me to learn HTML, CSS, and JavaScript beyond the classroom because every new feature creates another problem I have to understand and solve. My networking classes have also helped me better understand how the different parts of a web application communicate and depend on each other. Learning new coding languages does not come naturally to me, especially when it comes to syntax. I have to practice, build, break things, and work through the problem until I understand why it works. Connecting what I learn in class to something I am already building has made that process easier and has helped the concepts stick instead of feeling like information I only need for an assignment.
