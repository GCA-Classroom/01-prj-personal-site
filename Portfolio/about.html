About Me

I am a Human Systems Engineering UX major with a strong interest in how people interact with technology. My focus is on designing systems, interfaces, and digital experiences that are human-centered.

I believe technology should bend to the human — not the human to the technology. Through coursework and projects, I am learning to combine technical skills with an understanding of human behavior, cognition, and ergonomics.

My interests include user experience design, web development, and improving everyday systems by making them more intuitive, accessible, and enjoyable to use.
